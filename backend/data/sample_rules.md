# University Academic Rules

## Attendance
1. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to appear for the semester examination.
2. Students with an approved medical exemption may appear for the examination with at least 60% attendance.
3. Students below 50% attendance are not eligible for the examination regardless of exemption.
4. Medical exemption applications must be submitted within 7 days of returning to the university.

## Assignments
5. Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a late penalty of up to 10%.
6. An approved medical absence may qualify a student for an extended submission deadline.

## Re-examination
7. Students who fail a course may apply for the next available re-examination.
8. A student who has already passed a course may not use re-examination to improve the grade.

## Scholarship
9. Scholarship renewal requires a minimum CGPA of 7.5.
10. Students with an unresolved disciplinary case are not eligible for scholarship renewal.
11. Scholarship applications must be submitted before the published deadline.

## Fees and Hostel
12. Tuition fees paid for a completed semester are generally non-refundable.
13. Hostel security deposits are refundable after room inspection, subject to deductions for damage.
14. Hostel refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of checkout.

## Leave
15. Medical leave requires supporting documentation.
16. Leave approval does not automatically waive academic attendance requirements unless an applicable exemption is granted.

## Deliberate conflict for testing
17. The examination notice for one special program states that a minimum of 70% attendance is required.
18. The general academic regulation states that the same special program requires 65% attendance.

The system should flag rules 17 and 18 as a genuine unresolved conflict unless another rule establishes which document has priority.
